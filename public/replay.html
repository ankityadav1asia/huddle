Huddle

Mumbai Indians v Chennai Super Kings

Dream11 is now a second-screen app built around creator watch-alongs. Huddle is the AI layer inside that room: it keeps a busy chat readable and a quiet one alive, at a glance, while the match plays on the big screen.

  • Room pulseOne line on how the room feels, split by fan base.
  • Moment threadsArguments about a big moment get their own thread.
  • Smart pollsAsks the room at the right moment and remembers the answer.
  • Catch me upThree bullets when you look away from your phone.
  • Fans like youMatches you on team, players and votes.

The match is a simulated MI v CSK replay with real players, and the crowd is scripted Hinglish chat. The AI reads that chat and writes the pulse, threads, polls and catch-ups. Type in the room, in English or Hinglish, or ask @huddle a question.
